### How do I edit vanilla HUD elements?

You cannot. Imagine you are opening something like an image editing tool: what's already on-screen is a locked background layer. You add stuff on top of it, with complete freedom on making it look and work how you want.

You **can** turn vanilla HUD off though. See [How do I turn off vanilla HUD elements?](#how-do-i-turn-off-vanilla-hud-elements):

That being said, if you head to the [Block Library](concepts/block_library.md) (Blocks panel, top-left button labelled "Library"), under the HUD Studio category you'll find 1:1 recreations of certain vanilla elements, which you can modify every aspect of.

### How do I edit other mod's elements?

If the mod you're referring to was not made with HUD Studio, you cannot. That's not how this mod works.

If they were made with HUD Studio, you want to click on the element's row in the [Blocks panel](ui/blocks_tree.md), and click `Duplicate` in the element's **Properties** panel after. This unlocks the full edit form.

### How do I turn off vanilla HUD elements?

- In the [Blocks panel](ui/blocks_tree.md), look for the toolbar attached on its side.
- There's a button with a `` icon. Click it to open the `Toggle Vanilla HUD` panel.
- From here you can disable almost all elements by clicking on them in the panel.
- Any that cannot be replicated easily with HUD Studio (like the buffs bar, killfeed, etc) can be moved around by applying an offset.
