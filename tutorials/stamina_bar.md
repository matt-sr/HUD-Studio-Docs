A progress bar that follows your stamina.

1. In the [Blocks panel](../ui/blocks_tree.md)'s bottom toolbar, click **Add Block**.
2. With the block selected, click the **Add** button for `Progress Bar`.
3. In `Value`, set `Current` to `Data Source`, then pick `Player 1 (You)` > `Stamina`.
4. Set `Max` to `Data Source`, then pick `Player 1 (You)` > `Stamina Max`.
5. In `Style`, set `Size`, `Color` and `Background`.
6. Drag the bar into place on the canvas.
