A rectangle showing your equipped melee weapon.

1. In the [Blocks panel](../ui/blocks_tree.md)'s bottom toolbar, click **Add Block**.
2. With the block selected, click the **Add** button for `Rectangle`.
3. In `Value`, set `Material` to `Data Source`, then pick `Player 1 (You)` > `Melee Icon Texture`.
4. In `Style`, set `Size` to fit the icon.
5. Drag the icon into place on the canvas.
