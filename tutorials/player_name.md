A text node showing your character's name.

1. In the [Blocks panel](../ui/blocks_tree.md)'s bottom toolbar, click **Add Block**.
2. With the block selected, click the **Add** button for `Text`.
3. In `Value`, keep `Text` on `Single`.
4. Set it to `Data Source`, then pick `Player 1 (You)` > `Name`.
5. In `Style`, set `Font size` and `Color`.
6. Drag the text into place on the canvas.

For a teammate, pick `Player 2`, `Player 3` or `Player 4` in step 4.
