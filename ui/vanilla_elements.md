The **Toggle Vanilla Elements** panel hides or moves parts of the game's own HUD. Use it to clear room for your blocks, or to stop a vanilla element doubling up with one you built.

You cannot edit vanilla elements here, only hide or move them. For editable copies of some of them, see the HUD Studio category in the [Block Library](block_library.md).

## Opening it

Click the **Toggle Vanilla Elements** button on the Blocks panel's side toolbar.

## The map

The panel shows a 16:9 map of the screen. Each box on the map is one vanilla element, placed roughly where the game draws it.

| Box colour | Means                                    |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Light grey | shown                                    |
| Dark grey  | hidden                                   |
| Mid grey   | a group with some of its elements hidden |
| Blue       | moved away from its normal position      |

## Hiding elements

Click a box to hide that element. Click it again to show it.

| Element             | Notes                                       |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Player 1 Panel      | your own portrait, health and toughness     |
| Player 2 - 4 Panels | your teammates' panels                      |
| Dodge / Stamina     | can also be turned off in the game settings |
| Peril               | the psyker peril indicator                  |
| Subtitles           | can also be turned off in the game settings |
| Equipment           | the weapon and ammo panel                   |
| Ability             | the combat ability icon                     |

## Crosshair bars

The **Crosshair Bars** box has a caret. Click it to open a grid of the bars drawn around the crosshair:

- Weapon Charge-up Bar (power sword, etc.)
- Weapon Heat Bar (relic blade, etc.)
- Special Charge Bar (Mechanicus power sword, etc.)
- Force Greatsword (FGS) Charge Bar

Click a bar in the grid to hide or show it.

## Moving elements

Some elements are hard to rebuild with HUD Studio, so you can move them instead:

- Buffs
- Kill Feed
- Chat
- Objectives
- Objective Popup

Click one of these boxes to open its **X** and **Y** fields. They shift the element from where the game puts it, in pixels. Set both back to 0 to return it to its normal position.

## Saving

Your choices save with your HUD. They apply in game straight away, and stay after a restart.
